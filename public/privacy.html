BBL Studio / Business Brand Lab

Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

1. Who we are and how to contact us

BBL Studio, also known as Business Brand Lab, provides website design, UX/UI, branding and digital experience services at https://www.bbl.studio. This policy describes our website, consultation booking, business communications and connected business-management tools, including the Google consent application currently named “BBL Studio Booking” and its separate Growth Monitoring connection.

For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, contact hello@bbl.studio.

2. Information we process

Information you provide: contact details such as your name, business email, company, phone number, project requirements, consultation notes, messages and project correspondence. Booking records may include appointment time, time zone, attendees and scheduling identifiers.

Business prospect information: relevant public business contact details, business websites and observed website characteristics used to assess possible design or conversion improvements. We maintain outreach review records, delivery/reply records where connected, and suppression preferences.

Service and security records: request and execution logs, provider responses, errors, timestamps and records needed to secure and operate the service. Hosting and other service providers may process technical connection information, such as IP addresses, under their own policies.

Connected account information: only information made available through a permission you authorize, as described below. Authorization credentials are used on the server to maintain the authorized connection.

3. Google connections and permissions

Growth Monitoring uses a separate OAuth client from consultation booking. Its permissions are:

• https://www.googleapis.com/auth/webmasters.readonly — read Search Console information, including verified-site information and available search-performance reports such as queries, pages, clicks, impressions, position and click-through rate.

• https://www.googleapis.com/auth/analytics.readonly — read available Google Analytics property information and reports, including traffic sources, landing pages, sessions and key events.

Google grants these permissions across properties accessible to the authorizing account; the permission itself is not restricted to one property. Our monitoring configuration targets the bbl.studio Search Console property and the BBL Studio GA4 property. It does not require Gmail access, Google Calendar access, or permission to change Analytics or Search Console data. Offline authorization allows the server to refresh access when the owner is not using their computer.

Our separate consultation-booking integration may read calendar availability and relevant appointment records and create, update or cancel consultation events in the connected studio calendar. It processes appointment details to provide the booking service. The monitoring connection does not use the booking credential.

Creating an Analytics property or connecting the reporting API does not itself install a tracking tag on the website. Website analytics collection, if enabled, must be described in the website's actual tracking and consent disclosures. This policy does not imply that tracking is currently installed or that reports contain data.

4. Instagram connection

The configured Instagram read connection is for @businessbrandlab. It reads the authorized account identity and a limited media sample, including media identifiers, types and timestamps, to verify the account and support internal monitoring. This read connection is separate from publishing. The current read connection does not authorize sending direct messages or publishing posts.

5. How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, arrange consultations, deliver agreed services, manage project and sales records, prepare internal drafts, understand our own search and website performance, identify technical problems and protect the service. Owner review is required for outbound sales messages, publishing and commercial commitments within the growth workflows. Preparing a draft does not mean a message has been sent.

Google-connected data is used only for the disclosed booking and reporting functions. We do not use it for unrelated advertising, data brokerage or training general-purpose AI models. We do not sell Google user data. BBL Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements: https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy.

6. Storage, access and service providers

The service runs on server infrastructure independently of the owner's computer. OAuth credentials are stored in server-side secret configuration rather than website code or public repositories. Operational and business records may be stored in the service database; monitoring snapshots and execution logs support the owner dashboard and troubleshooting.

We use providers to host the application, store records, operate Google and Meta integrations and, where a feature is enabled, process email or payments. Providers receive information needed for their particular service; a feature being supported in code does not mean that every provider is connected or receives every category of information. Authorized personnel may access necessary records to deliver services, investigate issues or meet legal obligations. We may disclose information when legally required or to protect rights, safety and service security. We do not promise that any system is completely secure.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain information for the service, business records, security and applicable legal obligations. We do not claim a universal automatic deletion period: current monitoring snapshots may be replaced by later runs, while execution history, CRM records, provider logs and backups may have different retention behavior. You can request access, correction or deletion at hello@bbl.studio. We may need to verify your authority before acting. Applicable legal duties, legitimate recordkeeping needs and backup limitations may affect what can be removed and when.

Unsubscribe and suppression records may be retained in a minimal form to honor a request not to be contacted. Deleting a contact record should not cause an opted-out person to be contacted again.

8. Disconnecting Google and other choices

The account owner can revoke access through https://myaccount.google.com/connections. Revocation prevents further authorized API access but does not automatically erase information previously stored by BBL Studio or the provider. Contact hello@bbl.studio to request removal of stored connected-account information and credentials. Ask us to disconnect an integration if you no longer want it used. Access may also stop if a provider expires or revokes authorization.

You may decline optional information or authorization, although the relevant feature may then be unavailable. For business outreach, use the unsubscribe mechanism provided with a message or contact hello@bbl.studio to request suppression.

9. International processing and applicable rights

Our providers may process information in countries other than your own. Depending on applicable law and your circumstances, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection. Contact us to make a request. Rights depend on the applicable law and circumstances of your request.

10. Changes

We will update the policy when our practices change. A materially different use of Google-connected data requires updated disclosures and any consent required by Google before that new use begins.
