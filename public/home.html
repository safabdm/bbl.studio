A few useful details

Before we begin.

Do you offer website design in Orange County?

Yes. BBL Studio is an Orange County website design studio. We design and build custom landing pages, business websites, and commercial websites for businesses throughout Orange County, including Irvine, Newport Beach, Costa Mesa, Laguna Beach, and Tustin.

How much does website design cost?

BBL Studio provides a custom quote after consultation based on pages, functionality, and content readiness. Brand Identity is an optional add-on when needed.

Can you work with my existing brand?

Yes. A website engagement can build on your current identity. Brand Identity is an optional add-on when a stronger visual foundation is needed.

What determines the final scope and timeline?

The number of pages, functionality, content readiness, and feedback availability. We align on these during the initial conversation and confirm the scope before work begins.

What if I need more than ten pages or custom functionality?

We can discuss a custom scope for e-commerce, booking systems, memberships, multilingual websites, client portals, advanced integrations, or larger sites.

Are platform subscriptions included?

Platform costs are separate. Final pricing depends on the agreed scope, content, and functionality.
